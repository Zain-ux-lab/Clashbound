CLASHBOUND — CURRENT BATTLE RULES
Updated 14 October 2026

Choose three different characters and four moves each; both teams lock before reveal. First pick is the lead. Each side fields one active character.

Opening: higher lead Speed (+3 for Veyne) goes first. Tied leads compare total base team Speed. Only an exact tie uses an equal draw. Thereafter players alternate; Speed never adds turns.

Each normal turn uses one move or voluntarily switches. Switching uses the whole turn. A knockout replacement is free and preserves normal turn order. Drag Through the Thorns forces a free defender-choice replacement and also preserves their normal turn.

Each character has an automatic passive, a unique fourth move and one other fixed signature. Choose two flexible moves from the remaining options. All eight moves per character are immediately available. No mastery, progression unlocks or earned cosmetics.

Types: Magic > Elemental > Physical > Spirit > Magic. Strong attacks deal 125%; reverse 80%; equal/opposite 100%. Physical uses Power/Armour; others use Focus/Ward. Contact is independent of damage type.

Conditions, guard and passive state are visible. Ordinary switching clears conditions, copied spells, adaptation and previous-move restrictions. Pending wounds remain attached to Riftclaw and pay after its next normal action even if it switches. Glowmire's Last Light is once per battle.

A team loses when all three characters are knocked out. Simultaneous final knockouts do not reward the actor for sacrificing their final character. Reserve damage may knock out reserves without forcing an active replacement.

Exact abilities, starting numbers, timing, target choices and healing rules: frontend/guide.html and docs/CHARACTER_MECHANICS.md. Numbers remain provisional. There is no story campaign.
