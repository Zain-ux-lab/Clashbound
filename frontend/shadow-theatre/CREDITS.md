# Shadow Theatre homepage assets

Updated 29 October 2026.

The environment, Vesperfang and Bastion illustrations were generated for this project using the built-in image tool. The repaired Bastion artwork and original prompt records are included alongside the assets. These are illustrated 2D puppets, not full 3D character rigs. No artwork from other games is bundled.

The crest, side ruin renders, closing film and bat atlas were created in Blender for this project. The Hushwing silhouette and engraved button are original SVG artwork. The Accord combat sequence is an illustrated sparring loop, not recorded gameplay.

The original accord-forge.mp3 soundtrack uses synthesized instruments and no third-party samples. Metal Mania and Barlow retain their SIL Open Font License notices under assets/fonts. GSAP 3.13.0 and ScrollTrigger retain their distribution headers and licence URL under assets/vendor.

The existing battle, character pages and their separate asset credits remain applicable when following links from this homepage.
